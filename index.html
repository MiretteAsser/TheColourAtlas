Question 1 of 6 — Undertone
Look at the veins on your inner wrist. What colour are they?
Step outside or sit by a window. Avoid lamp or phone screen light — it distorts the colour.
Blue or purple
Distinctly blue-violet
Green or olive

How to read your veins

Go to a window in natural daylight. Hold your inner wrist up and look at the veins running lengthwise.

Blueish or purplish? That's a cool signal. Greenish or olive? That's warm. Can't decide? Pick "Mix of both."

Question 3 of 6 — Hair Depth
What is your natural hair colour (or before any colouring)?
Pick the closest match to your natural, uncoloured hair.
Picking your hair depth

If you colour your hair, think back to your natural colour before colouring. Gone grey? Pick the shade that best represents what your hair was in your 20s.

Question 4 of 6 — Eye Colour
What best describes your natural eye colour?
Look in natural light. Eye colour reveals both warmth and clarity.
Reading your eye colour

Look in natural light. Hazel eyes often appear to change — pick the colour that appears most often. Dark brown or near-black? Select the deepest brown option.

Question 5 of 6 — Natural Contrast
How much contrast is there between your natural hair and skin tone?
Compare your hair depth against your bare skin. Ignore highlights, makeup, or colouring.
Low contrast
Hair and skin close in depth — both light, or both medium
Medium contrast
Noticeable but not dramatic difference
High contrast
Striking difference — very dark hair on fair or medium skin
Measuring your natural contrast

This is purely about hair vs. bare skin depth — ignore eye colour and makeup. Low: both fair/light, or both medium-depth. High: one dramatically darker than the other.

Question 6 of 6 — Metal Test

Use real jewellery or fabric swatches — hold them under your chin in natural light, no makeup if possible.
Gold

How to do the metal test properly

Use real jewellery if you have it. Hold each metal under your chin in natural light only, no makeup if possible. Look for which makes skin look more alive.
